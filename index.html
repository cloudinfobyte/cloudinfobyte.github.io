Migration approach

Incremental by design, so the business keeps running

Big-bang rewrites fail. We move one capability at a time behind a stable interface, measure each step, and keep a clear path back.

Plan your migration
  1. AssessInventory services, dependencies, data flows, risks and cost. Agree success metrics.
  2. PlanTarget architecture, migration waves, rollback plan and a delivery roadmap.
  3. DecomposeCarve out services behind a gateway, split data ownership, add contract tests.
  4. AutomateInfrastructure as code, CI/CD and environment promotion for every service.
  5. OperateObservability, alerts, runbooks and a handover your team can run.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we have to rewrite our application to use microservices?

No. We usually start with the strangler pattern: new or extracted capabilities run as services behind a gateway while the monolith keeps serving the rest. You migrate in steps, not all at once.

Which cloud do you work with?

AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, plus hybrid and on-prem Kubernetes. Recommendations follow your workload, skills and cost goals, not a vendor preference.

Can you move us from Jenkins to GitHub Actions?

Yes. We inventory your existing jobs, port them to reusable workflows, run both in parallel, then cut over once results match.

What do alerts and monitoring cover?

Infrastructure, application and business metrics, centralised logs and traces, with alert rules tied to SLOs and routed to the right on-call channel.

Do you only build, or do you also operate?

Both are available. Many teams use us to build and hand over; others keep us on for ongoing operations and release support.
